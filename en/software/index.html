From napkin sketch to production

These phases aren't necessarily sequential — sometimes they run in parallel.

  1. Phase 1

    Discovery and architecture

    We analyze your process or business hypothesis. We define scope, architecture and tech stack, avoiding overengineering.

  2. Phase 2

    Development

    We work in stages and deliver value in each one. That way you can always see how it's shaping up, and we catch anything that isn't working early. We work side by side so there are no surprises.

  3. Phase 3

    Launch

    This phase includes training: if the product is an internal tool, your team will be the first to notice whether it works and when it fails. That's why we work with them to show them first-hand how it works.

  4. Phase 4

    Feedback

    For an internal tool, we gather the team's feedback; for a public product, we analyze the metrics. The idea is always the same: adjust, fix and scale. The tool has to fit the team, and the product has to fit its audience.
